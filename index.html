CF-01 Shipped Spring 2026

Portfolio Tracker App

Class project, Financial Data Analytics II

Problem
Comparing portfolios on return alone hides the risk taken to earn it.
What I did
Built and deployed a Streamlit app in Python: Sharpe and Sortino ratios, rolling volatility and drawdowns, correlation and covariance matrices, and Markowitz optimization for the global minimum-variance and tangency portfolios along the efficient frontier, with per-asset risk contribution, a weight-slider builder and estimation-window sensitivity.
Result
A live app anyone can open and test against a multi-asset portfolio.

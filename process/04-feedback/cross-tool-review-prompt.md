Review this personal portfolio site: https://juanpablohoyosc.github.io and its phone page https://juanpablohoyosc.github.io/links/

I'm a finance and financial analytics student graduating in December 2026, looking for financial or data analyst roles. The site is aimed at recruiters who find me at a career fair, on LinkedIn, or before an interview.

Answer these, in this order, and be blunt:

1. Read only the first screen on a phone. What do you know about me, and what are you still missing?
2. Which of the four case files is strongest, which is weakest, and why?
3. Where does the writing sound machine-made rather than like a person?
4. What would stop you from contacting me?
5. Name the three changes that would most improve my chances with a recruiter, in priority order.

Don't praise the design. I want the parts that don't work.
